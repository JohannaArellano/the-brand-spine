## Brand Context

**The Brand Spine** is a brand strategy and identity firm founded by Johanna Arellano.
It serves established business owners, real estate professionals, and executives who need to build or sharpen their authority brand.

**ICP:** Business owners with proven revenue ($500K–$5M+) who are ready to be seen as the definitive expert in their field.

**Brand Voice:** Calm. Grounded. Direct. Structured clarity without hype or urgency.
Never: fluffy language, vague promises, luxury jargon, influencer tone, or performative warmth.

**Tagline direction:** Clarity-first. Authority-led. Identity-driven.

---

## Website Purpose

The website serves three functions:
1. **Authority signal** — establishes Jo as the go-to brand strategist for serious operators
2. **Lead qualification** — filters for right-fit clients before they inquire
3. **Content hub** — will house SEO/GEO/AEO-optimized articles and thought leadership

---

## SEO / GEO / AEO Priorities

- Optimize for **semantic search and AI-cited content** (GEO = Generative Engine Optimization)
- Target long-tail authority queries: brand strategy for [niche], how to build a personal brand as [ICP], etc.
- Structure content so it can be directly quoted by AI assistants (clear definitions, structured answers, no filler)
- Every page should have a clear primary keyword, proper heading hierarchy, and meta description
